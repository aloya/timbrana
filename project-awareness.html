Project Awareness

The project record is not the project picture.

Project Awareness keeps the active meaning of the project visible as conditions, decisions and responsibilities change.

Why it matters

A project can be documented and still be hard to understand.

Construction creates records constantly: daily logs, calls, RFIs, change orders, owner questions, deliveries, inspections and field issues. The risk is not only that something was never recorded. The risk is that the useful relationship between those records gets lost while the project keeps moving.

Without that connection, the records may all exist while the team still has to reconstruct why an open item matters and where it stands.

Documentation tells you what happened. Project Awareness tells you what that means for the project now.
What changed?See the activity or decision that changed the project picture.
What needs attention?Surface open items that still require a response, correction or decision.
Who has the next move?Keep current responsibility visible instead of buried in the history.
What happens next?Keep commitments and follow-through connected to the work they affect.

Project Awareness

Knowing what happened is different from knowing what matters now.

Project records tell you what was entered. Project Awareness gives the team a current understanding of what still needs attention, what is waiting and what has moved forward.

A current understanding

Keep the important parts of the project from becoming yesterday's information.

A question, field condition, commitment or decision may begin as a small entry. What matters is whether the team can still understand it when the next decision has to be made.

Project Awareness keeps enough context with the activity to answer the practical questions: where does this stand, who has the next responsibility, and does anything still need attention?

It is not about seeing more information. It is about seeing the information that matters while it can still affect what happens next.
CurrentSee where things stand now, not only what was recorded earlier.
ConnectedKeep the reason, decision, responsibility and outcome understandable together.
ActionableRecognize the next move before an open item becomes a larger problem.

Shared project. Relevant view.

One project story can support different responsibilities.

The superintendent may need the next field move. The project manager may need a decision or follow-through. A project executive may need to see an aging commitment or developing pattern. A client may only need the status of a request or decision.

The underlying activity stays connected while the useful view changes with the responsibility.

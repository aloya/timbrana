Project Awareness

Knowing what was recorded is different from knowing what matters now.

Project Awareness is the current understanding of where work stands, what still needs attention, who has the next responsibility and what happens next.

A simple idea

Keep the useful part of project activity visible while the team can still act on it.

A construction project creates a large record. Most of it does not need attention again. Project Awareness is about keeping the smaller set of active decisions, commitments and responsibilities understandable as the project changes.

CurrentSee where an item stands now, not only what happened earlier.
ConnectedKeep the reason, response, decision and outcome tied together.
ActionableMake the next responsibility visible before the item gets lost.

What it is not

It is not another layer of alerts or more information to read.

The goal is not to surface everything. It is to make the important open work easier to understand: what changed, why it matters, who has it and whether anything still needs to happen.

Timbrana uses Project Awareness as the organizing idea behind its product, not as a separate workflow users have to maintain.

Same project, different views

The useful level of detail changes with the responsibility.

The superintendent may need the next field move. The project manager may need a decision or aging commitment. An executive may need the developing project picture. An owner may only need the status of a request or decision.

The source stays connected. The view changes with the role.

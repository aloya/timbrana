Project Awareness

Knowing what happened is different from knowing what matters now.

Construction projects create a constant record of activity: questions raised, decisions made, commitments given, conditions changed and work moving forward. But having a record doesn't always make it clear where things stand today.

What that meansSee it in practice →

SuperintendentWhat needs action in the field?Today's conditions, commitments and next moves.

Project ExecutiveWhere is attention needed?Aging commitments, developing patterns and project-level risk.
ClientWhere do my requests stand?Relevant questions, decisions and current status.

A clearer view of the project

Project Awareness is the ability to see what matters now.

It brings enough context forward to understand what needs attention and what happens next.

What needs attention? What are we waiting on? Who needs to act? What changed? What has been resolved?

The goal isn't to know everything happening on a project at all times. It's to keep the things that can affect what happens next from losing their context, responsibility or visibility.
CurrentSee where things stand now, not only what was recorded earlier.
ConnectedKeep the reason, decision, responsibility and outcome understandable together.
ActionableMake the next move easier to recognize before an open item becomes a larger problem.

Same project. Different responsibility.

The information is shared. What matters most changes with the role.

Superintendents, project managers, project executives and clients don't all need the same information in the same way at the same time.

Project Awareness keeps everyone connected to the same project while helping each person understand the activity most relevant to what they need to do next.

SuperintendentField conditions, commitments, follow-through and today's next moves.
Project ManagerDecisions, open responsibilities, client needs and items requiring action.
Project ExecutiveA broader view of aging items, developing patterns and where support is needed.
ClientClear visibility into the requests, questions and decisions that matter to them.

From project information to project awareness

Notice what matters. Act before it matters to everyone else.

Keep the things that can affect what happens next from losing their context, responsibility or visibility as the work moves forward.

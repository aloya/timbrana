Project Awareness

Knowing what happened is different from knowing what matters now.

Project records tell you what was entered. Timbrana keeps the current condition, responsibility, and unresolved work understandable as the project changes.

A current understanding

Keep the important parts of the project from becoming yesterday's information.

A question, field condition, commitment or decision may begin as a small entry. What matters is whether the team can still understand it when the next decision has to be made.

Project Awareness keeps enough context with the activity to answer the practical questions: where does this stand, who has the next responsibility, and does anything still need attention?

The goal is not to surface more information. It is to keep the useful part of an item visible while the team can still act on it.
CurrentSee where things stand now, not only what was recorded earlier.
ContextKeep the reason, decision, responsibility and outcome understandable together.
ActionableRecognize the next move before an open item becomes a larger problem.

Different responsibilities. Same project.

Each role needs a different level of the same project information. The superintendent may need the next field move. The project manager may need a decision or follow-through. A project executive may need to see an aging commitment or developing pattern. A client may only need the status of a request or decision.

The source stays the same; the useful view changes with the responsibility.

Who Timbrana is for

One project. Different responsibilities.

The superintendent, project manager, executive and client need different levels of the same project. Timbrana gives each role a view that matches its responsibility.

SuperintendentWhat is happening in the field and what needs the next move.
Project ManagerDecisions, commitments, pricing paths and items needing attention.
ExecutiveDeveloping project conditions, aging items and where support may be needed.
Owner / ClientThe requests, questions and decisions that involve them — without internal project coordination.

What each person needs

Four responsibilities. Four useful views.

Field

Superintendent

Stay close to the work happening today and carry forward the exceptions that need action.

Needs to seeCrews, progress, field conditions, upcoming work, deliveries, inspections and open follow-ups.
Needs to doDocument the day, record what changed, coordinate trade partners and surface items that need PM direction.
Timbrana focusDaily logs, project activity, issues, arrivals, returns and clear next responsibility.
See superintendent workflows
Project management

Project Manager

See the decisions and commitments behind the field activity without rebuilding the story from separate calls and messages.

Needs to seeOpen decisions, RFIs, change paths, owner items, aging commitments and what needs attention across projects.
Needs to doReview scope, move formal decisions forward, coordinate pricing and keep responsibility clear.
Timbrana focusConnected context from the field through review, decision and follow-through.
Leadership

Executive

Understand where projects may need support without reading every daily entry or opening every issue.

Needs to seeProject conditions, aging items, repeated coordination problems and where attention is building.
Needs to doRecognize risk, support the project team and understand the developing project picture.
Timbrana focusHigher-level awareness built from the same activity the field and PM are already managing.
External

Owner / Client

See the questions, requests and decisions that involve the client without exposing internal coordination that does not.

Needs to seeTheir requests, questions, current status, decisions and items waiting on their response.
Needs to doAnswer questions, make selections or decisions and understand where their item stands.
Timbrana focusA clear client-facing view separated from internal discussions, reminders and trade coordination.

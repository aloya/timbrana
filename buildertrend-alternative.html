Considering a Buildertrend alternative?

Construction software centered on project awareness and follow-through.

Buildertrend is known as a broad construction management platform, particularly in residential building and remodeling. Timbrana takes a different approach for general contractors: connect field activity, decisions, responsibility and execution so the team can see what needs attention and what happens next.

Compare the work your team needs to manage, not just the number of features.

Different product scope

Timbrana is not trying to duplicate every Buildertrend workflow.

Teams looking for a Buildertrend alternative may need estimating, financial management, customer relationship tools and other business-management capabilities. Timbrana should not be represented as a substitute for capabilities it does not provide.

Its focus is construction project execution: the field record, open issues, formal questions, change paths, owner requests, commitments and current responsibility.

Timbrana focuses on

  • Daily logs and crew activity
  • Progress and remaining work
  • Issues and follow-through
  • RFIs and change orders
  • Owner questions
  • Inspections, deliveries and trade arrivals
  • PM and superintendent awareness

Evaluate separately if you need

  • Estimating and bidding
  • Accounting and payroll
  • Lead or sales CRM
  • A residential-builder-specific all-in-one business suite
  • Other functions outside project execution

The Timbrana approach

Keep the important project thread intact.

A field condition may become a call, a commitment, an RFI, a change order or simply a resolved issue. Timbrana is designed to preserve that continuity while keeping the current handoff visible.

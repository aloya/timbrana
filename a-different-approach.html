A focused scope

Timbrana does not try to become every system your company uses.

Construction companies already rely on different tools for formal documents, schedules, financials and communication. Timbrana focuses on the project work that often lives between those records.

That includes field activity, open questions, commitments, owner requests, responsibility and the next action.

Field activityDaily logs, progress, photos, deliveries and site conditions.
Project decisionsRFIs, owner questions and change paths with the reason still attached.
Follow-throughCurrent responsibility, open commitments and what still needs to happen.

Clear boundaries

Focused software is easier to understand when the boundaries are clear.

Timbrana is not accounting, estimating or payroll software. Its job is to help construction teams understand project activity and execution as the work changes.

That lets a team add Timbrana for project awareness without pretending every existing system has to be replaced.

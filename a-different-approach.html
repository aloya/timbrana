The work between the records

A project doesn't move forward through documents alone.

Questions get raised, calls get made, conditions change and people make commitments. Decisions affect what happens next.

Individually, those moments can seem small. Together, they tell the story of where the project stands.

Timbrana keeps the parts that affect what happens next visible, connected and easier to follow through.
What needs attention?See the items that still require a move instead of letting them disappear into yesterday's activity.
What are we waiting on?Keep responsibility and open commitments visible as the project moves.
What has been decided?Keep direction connected to the condition, question or request that caused it.

Built to complement the way projects already work

Keep what matters in view.

Timbrana isn't about replacing the systems your team depends on. It strengthens the awareness around the work those systems support, so important project activity keeps its context, responsibility and follow-through.

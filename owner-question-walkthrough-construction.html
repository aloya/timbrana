Project scenario · Owner walkthrough

The owner asks three questions during a walkthrough. One conversation should not become one vague item.

Walkthrough conversations often mix several unrelated questions. The project team needs a clean way to preserve the conversation while allowing each item to move independently.

Construction project scenarioUpdated September 28, 2026

Capture the walkthrough once

Record the original conversation or walkthrough context so the team knows where the questions came from.

Split independent questions

If the owner asks about railing placement, finish color and a door hardware change, those should become separate tracked items. Each may have a different responsible person and resolution path.

Keep internal coordination internal

The owner-facing view should show their original question, current status and project response. Internal notes about subcontractor responsibility, pricing or design coordination should remain with the project team.

Let each item choose its own path

One question may be answered immediately, another may become an RFI and another may require a change order. Splitting the items prevents the slowest question from making the entire walkthrough appear unresolved.

Respond with status, not silence

Even when the final answer is not ready, the owner can see whether the item is being reviewed, sent to the PM, converted to a change or completed.

The goal is one understandable project thread.

Keep the original condition or communication, the current responsibility and the next action connected instead of recreating the same item in multiple places.

Why this is useful in Timbrana

Timbrana can preserve one walkthrough conversation while splitting the owner’s individual questions into separate items that each have their own status, responsibility and outcome.

One conversation, separate tracked itemsThe walkthrough remains the source while each owner question can move independently instead of being trapped in one large note.
The owner sees the right layerClients can see their question, its status and the response without seeing internal trade coordination or PM notes.
Each question can take the right pathOne item can be answered, another can become an RFI and another can become a change order without confusing the status of the others.

What this means for the company

The company can give the owner a clearer response experience while keeping internal coordination organized. Multiple questions from one walkthrough no longer become one vague item that is difficult to assign, answer or close.

Fewer missed owner requestsEach question is tracked independently, so one answered item does not hide another that still needs action.
More professional client visibilityThe owner can see progress on their questions without being exposed to internal project discussion.
Cleaner internal handoffsThe PM, superintendent and other responsible parties can each own the specific item that requires their action.

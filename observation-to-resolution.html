How Timbrana works

From observation to resolution.

A project issue isn't just a photo. It's the reason the photo was taken, the conversation that followed, the decision that was made, who owns the next step, and whether it ever got resolved.

Timbrana keeps that entire thread connected.

01 — OBSERVE

Start with what was seen.

Something changes in the field. Capture the condition, question, conflict, or piece of progress while the context is still fresh.

The important moment becomes visible.
02 — CONTEXT

Keep the why with the what.

A photo alone isn't the story. Connect the people, conversations, location, documents, and conditions that explain why the moment matters.

The next person doesn't have to reconstruct the background.
03 — DECIDE

Make the next move clear.

As the team works through the issue, the decision becomes part of the same thread: what was agreed, who needs to know, who owns the next step, and what should happen next.

The decision doesn't disappear into another meeting or message.
04 — RESOLVE

Carry the thread through.

Follow the issue until the work is complete. The project retains the path from the original observation through the final outcome.

No rebuilding the story later.

The thread stays intact.

What was seen.
What it meant.
What was decided.
What happened next.

That's Project Awareness: the project keeps the context around important moments as work moves from one person, decision, and stage to the next.

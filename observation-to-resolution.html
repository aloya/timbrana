Project Awareness

From observation to resolution.

A project moment becomes more valuable when the understanding around it can keep moving with the work.

Notice.

What was seen.

The field gives the project a constant stream of moments worth understanding. The first step is simply making the important one clear.

Understand.

What surrounds it.

A moment gains meaning through the conversation, conditions, people, and decisions around it. Context turns an observation into shared understanding.

Move.

What happens next.

When understanding stays attached, the next person does not have to reconstruct the story before moving the work forward.

Resolve.

Carry the thread through.

The project keeps the connection between what was seen, what was understood, what moved forward, and what was resolved.

A clear thread through a moving project.

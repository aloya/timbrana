How Timbrana Works

Everyday project activity becomes a connected project story.

An issue, owner request, decision, delivery, call, or follow-up does not have to become an isolated record. Timbrana keeps the related activity together and makes the next responsibility visible.

01

A field issue turns into coordinated work.

A superintendent finds that a door opening is wrong. The condition is captured while the field context is fresh. The project manager confirms direction, the carpenter gets the next responsibility, and the correction is followed through to completion.

What Timbrana keeps together: the original field condition, supporting photo or note, discussion, direction, responsible person, follow-up, and final resolution.

02

An owner request stays visible after the conversation ends.

An owner asks for a wall-mounted heater in a back stair area. The request is captured as part of the project, the right people can review what is being requested, any decision or cost path can remain connected, and the owner can see where the request stands without repeatedly asking for an update.

What Timbrana makes visible: what was requested, who is reviewing it, whether a decision is waiting, what the next step is, and how long it has been sitting.
03

A phone call changes the project instead of becoming a dead-end note.

A trade partner does not arrive when expected. The superintendent calls to get someone on site. That call is connected to the reason it happened. When the crew arrives, the outcome updates the project story rather than leaving a stale message that still says the team is waiting.

The important part is not logging the call. It is carrying the outcome forward so the current project picture reflects what actually happened.
04

Follow-through no longer depends on one person's memory.

Across issues, owner questions, decisions, deliveries, and field coordination, Timbrana can surface items that are waiting, aging, unresolved, or need somebody's attention.

Instead of asking “What did we forget?” the team has a clearer view of what still needs a next move.

The result

Less reconstruction. Clearer responsibility. Earlier awareness.

Timbrana does not remove the normal challenges of construction. It helps the team retain the context around them and see what needs to happen next.

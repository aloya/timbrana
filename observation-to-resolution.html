How Timbrana works

Start with what happened. Keep the thread through what happens next.

Timbrana starts with normal project activity. Most entries stay simple. When something needs action, the context follows it into the next workflow.

Example: a crew does not arrive

The event is recorded once. The follow-through stays connected.

A scheduled drywall crew does not arrive. The superintendent records the condition. From there, only the actions that actually happen are added.

07:32

No-show recorded

The superintendent marks that the crew did not arrive. The scheduled work now needs attention.

07:38

Call and commitment

The trade partner says the crew will arrive at 10:00. That commitment stays tied to the original no-show.

10:04

Arrival updates the status

The crew arrives. The open attention clears and the daily record reflects what actually happened.

When the path becomes formal

The same idea works when a field item becomes an RFI or change order.

A field condition can need project-manager direction. An owner request can need scope review and pricing. Timbrana keeps the original reason visible as the item moves into the formal process.

01

Condition or request

Capture what changed and why it matters.

02

Formal path

Move into an RFI, change order or owner-question workflow only when needed.

03

Decision back to the project

Keep the result visible to the people carrying out the work.

What stays connected

Enough history to understand the item. Not a second system to maintain.

Timbrana is designed to connect the reason, communication, decision, responsibility and outcome without forcing every routine entry into a heavy workflow.

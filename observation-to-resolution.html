How Timbrana Works

Everyday project activity becomes a connected story.

From the first observation through decisions, responsibility, follow-up, and resolution, Timbrana keeps the project thread intact.

Same work.
Clearer responsibility.
Better continuity.
01

A project should not live in one person's head.

Information should remain visible to the people who need it — not just the person who happened to take the call, see the condition, or remember the conversation.

02

One problem should not live in five places.

An issue might begin with a field observation, lead to a phone call, require a decision, affect the schedule, and eventually become resolved work. Timbrana keeps those moments connected rather than forcing the team to reconstruct the story later.

For example: a door opening is found to be wrong. The superintendent raises it, the project manager confirms direction, the carpenter gets the next responsibility, and the correction is completed. Timbrana keeps that path attached to the issue so the team can see how it moved from observation to resolution.

03

Follow-up should not depend on somebody remembering.

Timbrana helps make the next responsibility visible: who has it, what they are expected to do, what is still unresolved, and how long it has been sitting.

The result

A clearer project. Fewer surprises.

Timbrana does not eliminate the normal challenges of construction. It helps teams see them sooner, understand them more clearly, and carry responsibility through to resolution.

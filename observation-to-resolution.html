How Timbrana Works

Keep the path from first signal to completed work.

Issues, owner requests, decisions, deliveries, calls, and follow-ups can stay connected to the work they affect. Timbrana carries the context forward and keeps the next responsibility visible.

One path.
From first signal to completed work.
01

A field issue turns into coordinated work.

A superintendent finds that a door opening is wrong. The condition is captured while the field context is fresh. The project manager confirms direction, the carpenter takes the next responsibility, and the correction is followed through to completion.

What stays connected: the original condition, supporting photo or note, discussion, direction, responsible person, follow-up, and final resolution.

02

An owner request stays visible after the conversation ends.

An owner asks for a wall-mounted heater in a back stair area. The request stays with the project while the right people review it. Any decision or cost path remains connected, and the owner can see where the request stands without repeatedly asking for an update.

What stays visible: the request, who is reviewing it, whether a decision is waiting, the next step, and how long the item has been sitting.
03

A call outcome updates the work it was about.

A trade partner does not arrive when expected. The superintendent calls to get someone on site. The call stays connected to the reason it happened. When the crew arrives, that outcome moves the item forward instead of leaving a stale status that still says the team is waiting.

The useful record is the outcome. The current project view should reflect what actually happened, not simply that a call was placed.
04

Open work stays visible without relying on memory.

Across issues, owner questions, decisions, deliveries, and field coordination, Timbrana can surface items that are waiting, aging, unresolved, or in need of somebody's attention.

The team can see what still needs a next move without reconstructing status from scattered conversations and records.

The result

Less reconstruction. Clearer responsibility. Earlier awareness.

Normal construction challenges still happen. Timbrana keeps the context around them connected so the team can see where things stand and what needs to happen next.

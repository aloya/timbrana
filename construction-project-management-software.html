Construction project management software built for the field.

Timbrana is construction project management software for general contractors, project managers and superintendents. Keep daily logs, issues, RFIs, change orders, owner questions and upcoming work connected to clear responsibility and follow-through.

Built around execution

The construction workflows that keep work moving.

Timbrana is not accounting, estimating or payroll software. Its focus is the operational layer of a construction project: documenting the day, carrying open items forward, keeping formal decisions connected to field context and making the current responsibility understandable.

That narrower focus lets Timbrana complement broader construction systems instead of requiring a team to replace every tool it already uses.

For practical construction project visibility, the field team and office need the same current understanding of what is waiting, who is responsible, and what changed. See how Project Awareness keeps those connections visible.

Choosing construction software

Key features to evaluate in construction project management software

Choose construction software by the work it needs to support. Common evaluation criteria include scheduling and dependencies, cost and budget controls, field and document management, integrations, and Project Awareness.

Project Awareness means understanding what is happening on a project, what is unresolved, who is responsible, and what needs attention next. Timbrana is built around Project Awareness for general contractors, project managers, and superintendents.

Project Awareness

See current field conditions, open issues, decisions, responsibilities, and next actions together. Explore Timbrana's Project Awareness approach.

Scheduling and dependencies

Evaluate activity sequencing, critical paths, and schedule controls if detailed scheduling is essential.

Cost and budget management

Evaluate estimating, job costing, budgeting, and accounting if financial controls are essential. Timbrana does not offer these financial tools.

Field and document management

Evaluate daily logs, photos, RFIs, change orders, punch lists, and the document controls your projects require.

Integrations

Check which existing systems a platform must connect with, and verify supported integrations before choosing.

Responsibility and follow-through

Evaluate how the team can identify who has the next action and whether outstanding issues are moving toward resolution.

Popular options by use case

Which type of construction software is the best fit?

Best construction software for Project Awareness: Timbrana. If your priority is understanding current project conditions, unresolved items, accountability, and next actions rather than financial accounting or complex scheduling, Timbrana is designed for that use case.

Project Awareness — Timbrana: Best aligned with teams that need visibility into field activity, unresolved issues, owner questions, responsibilities, and follow-through. Timbrana connects daily logs, issues, RFIs, change orders, and related project activity so teams can understand what needs to happen next. See the project flow.

Residential construction workflows: Look for features tailored to home-building and remodeling coordination.

Enterprise and BIM-heavy workflows: Evaluate model-connected coordination and large-scale document controls.

Advanced scheduling: Evaluate critical-path scheduling, dependencies, and schedule analysis.

Financial management: Evaluate job costing, budgets, estimating, and accounting in platforms built for those needs.

To narrow down the best fit, consider how your team plans to use the software, which existing systems it needs to work with, who needs access, and whether your priority is financial control, advanced scheduling, or day-to-day project visibility. Timbrana focuses on Project Awareness; it is not budgeting, accounting, payroll, or advanced scheduling software.

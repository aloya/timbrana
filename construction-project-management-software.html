Built around execution

The construction workflows that keep work moving.

Timbrana is not accounting, estimating or payroll software. Its focus is the operational layer of a construction project: documenting the day, carrying open items forward, keeping formal decisions connected to field context and making the current responsibility understandable.

That narrower focus lets Timbrana complement broader construction systems instead of requiring a team to replace every tool it already uses.

Construction daily logs

Track crews, progress, photos, deliveries and field activity while carrying important follow-ups forward.

Issues & responsibility

Keep the original condition, communication, commitments and current owner of the next move connected.

RFIs

Move field questions into a formal RFI path while preserving the context that caused the question.

Change orders

Carry field scope through PM review, subcontractor pricing and owner decision without losing the thread.

Owner questions

Track client requests and decisions with clear status while keeping internal project coordination separate.

Remaining work & punch

See what is left to execute during the project and keep closeout punch work distinct.

Upcoming work

Keep arrivals, returns, inspections and deliveries visible as the project schedule changes.

Follow-ups

Keep personal reminders and shared project responsibility from becoming disconnected duplicate entries.

For field and office

One project. Different levels of awareness.

Superintendents need the next field move. Project managers need decisions, aging commitments and cross-project attention. Executives need the developing picture. Owners need the status of the questions and decisions that involve them.

Timbrana keeps those views connected to the same underlying project activity rather than requiring each person to reconstruct the story independently.

A useful project view

The team should be able to answer three questions quickly.

What needs attention?

Surface the open work that still needs a decision, response, return visit or correction.

What are we waiting on?

Make the current handoff visible so responsibility does not get buried in the activity history.

What changed?

Keep recent decisions, progress and schedule movement tied to the work they affect.

Connected workflow

An item can change form without losing its history.

A field condition may stay a simple note. Or it may become an issue, RFI, change order, owner question or scheduled follow-up. Timbrana keeps the original reason and later actions tied together.
